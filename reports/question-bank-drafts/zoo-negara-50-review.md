# Zoo Negara Malaysia — 50 multiple-choice questions

First destination batch of the requested 62-record project. Draft for review; not inserted into Supabase. Researched 4 September 2026 using the zoo's official pages. Exhibit questions reflect the published descriptions, not a guarantee every exhibit is open on a visitor's day. Prices, feeding schedules, current headcounts and unverified opening claims are excluded.
