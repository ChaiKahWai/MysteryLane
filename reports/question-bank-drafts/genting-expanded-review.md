# Genting Highlands — expanded question review

10 draft multiple-choice questions. Not inserted into the database. This expanded review supersedes Genting's three-question starter section in the earlier review files. Other destinations have not been expanded in this update.

## 1. Which Malaysian state contains the Genting Highlands resort area?

A. Pahang

B. Perlis

C. Johor

D. Melaka

Answer: Pahang

Hint 1: It is an east-coast Peninsular state.

Hint 2: Kuantan is its capital.

Hint 3: The correct answer is Pahang.
